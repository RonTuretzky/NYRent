How it works

Five steps, two roles, one email. Every step after series creation is a permissionless on-chain transaction.

1

Sponsor funds

The sponsor collateralizes a series: strikes $88.00 → $96.00, premium 28.50% of max claim, capped capacity. Collateral is reserved — issuance can never exceed it.

2

Buyer mints cover

Pay the premium in WXDAI, mint ERC-1155 cover units: 1 unit = 1 wei of max claim. Terms are immutable from the first sale — no setter even exists.

3

The email arrives

CRE Daily's Market Snapshot prints the Manhattan average effective rent. Its DKIM signature makes those bytes verifiable by anyone, forever.

4

On-chain DKIM settlement

Anyone submits the raw email. The contract checks the RSA-2048 signature, body hash, tag policy and template, extracts the rent, and fixes the payout ratio — once.

5

Redeem

Burn cover units for amount × ratio. Redemption is never pausable. After the redeem window, residual reserves release to the sponsor.

Settlement rules

The full statement with trust boundaries is in PROTOCOL.md. Green rules are the oracle's email acceptance; blue rules are the pool's money math.

  1. New email only: emailId = sha256(body) never seen before (replays revert).
  2. Signed headers contain a from: line with <mail@newyork.credaily.com> and end with the canonical dkim-signature: line.
  3. Strict tag policy: v=1, a=rsa-sha256, c=relaxed/relaxed, d=newyork.credaily.com, s=b37, empty b=, no l=, duplicates revert.
  4. The signed bh= equals the base64 SHA-256 of the submitted body.
  5. The signed timestamp is not in the future (t ≤ now + 1 day).
  6. RSA-2048 PKCS#1 v1.5 verification against the immutable pinned modulus (keccak: 0x2f2f9938…4a41).
  7. Template extraction: exactly one Manhattan Office Rent anchor, then Avg Effective, then $NN.NN / SF within bounded distance — quoted-printable decoded on the fly.
  8. A series settles against an observation whose signed email time lies in [obsStart, obsEnd]; first successful settle wins, one-shot.
  9. Payout ratio = clamp((cents − 8800) / (9600 − 8800), 0, 1) for the demo strikes; $92.88 ⇒ 61%.
  10. Solvency invariant: reserved claims never exceed pool balance; the sponsor can only withdraw free capital; pause never blocks redeem.

Payout curve

Linear between the strikes, clamped outside them. The dot marks the 2026-09-17 authenticated observation.
